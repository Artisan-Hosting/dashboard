What do you want to do?

Run an app or website

Put a project online on machines we monitor. Deploy it from git, and change its size when it needs more.

You can change: instances, environments, memory, processor, deploy branch, git source

Own a domain

Register one, or bring your own. We do the DNS and the certificate.

You can change: register or bring, which project it points at, auto-renew, who manages DNS

Get email

Mailboxes on your own domain, with webmail and storage that we watch for you.

You can change: mailboxes, storage each, warning level, DNS records

Run open-source software

Ghost, Plausible, Uptime Kuma, Shlink and PostHog, installed and kept up to date.

You can change: the app, a staging copy, its address, single sign-on, outgoing email

Rent a machine or a GPU

A full virtual machine, or a GPU for as long as you need one.

You can change: power state, GPU memory, model, idle timeout

How this console works

The data is made up

Everything you see here is a sample. Nothing you change is saved or sent anywhere.

Apply shows its work

Before anything changes you get a list of exactly what would happen. Signed out, it stops there.

Signing in swaps the data

The screens stay the same. Your own projects, domains and usage replace the samples.
